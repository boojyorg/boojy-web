Website

boojy.org counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites or build a profile of you. There are no accounts and no ads.

Boojy Audio

Local storage

All projects and settings are stored on your computer. Nothing is uploaded to the cloud.

Crash reports (opt-in)

You can choose to send anonymous crash reports. They include the error and where it happened, and your OS and app version. They never include personal data, project files or audio. You choose on first launch.

Update checker

The app can check for updates. This only sends your current version, to see if a newer one exists.

Boojy Notes

Local storage

Notes are plain files in a folder you choose on your device. Nothing is sent to Boojy. If a cloud service you use syncs that folder, that service's own privacy policy applies.

Update checker

Notes checks for new versions and installs them automatically. You can turn this off in Settings → Updates.

Your rights (GDPR)

You can ask for a copy of any data I hold about you, or ask me to delete it. Email tyr@boojy.org.

Data sharing

I don't sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone.

Changes to this policy

If Boojy ever collects more data, for example if accounts or cloud sync are added, I'll update this page before that happens.
